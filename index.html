About B&E Co.

Built at the intersection of energy equipment and petrochemical trade

BanejEnergy (B&E Co.) supplies the equipment that generates power and the commodities that feed industrial processes — solar panels, gas turbines, and generators alongside urea, sulfur, naphtha, and LPG.

Operating across two supply chains lets us serve utilities, EPC contractors, fertilizer producers, and industrial buyers from a single point of contact, without compromising on sourcing standards in either market.

Every shipment — whether a turbine component or a cargo of naphtha — is sourced, inspected, and documented against the same requirements our clients depend on.
